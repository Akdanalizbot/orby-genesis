# Character Story

ORBY was born from the very first spark of energy on Robinhood Chain.

Unlike ordinary creatures, ORBY is made entirely of living energy.

Every explorer who joins the community makes ORBY shine brighter.

ORBY doesn't gain power from money.

ORBY gains power from people, creativity and community.

As the universe grows...

ORBY evolves.
